How the deck works

weighted, not random
  • Straight from the lessons. Every card is extracted verbatim from the sections' self-check panels. If you edit a self-check in a lesson, regenerate assets/drill-data.js and the deck updates automatically.
  • Spaced-repetition scheduling. Each card tracks its own review date based on your net score (correct answers minus misses). The first rung is 1 day, then 4 days, climbing further the cleaner your record is and capping at three weeks. Missing a card drops it one rung and puts it back in line right away, and your next right answer earns that rung back, so a slip is a small bump, not a full reset. The due for review counter only counts cards you've already seen whose rest window is up; brand-new cards wait behind them rather than piling up as a backlog. Each session pulls your most overdue cards first, then mixes in unseen ones.
  • Smart weighting on top. Cards you miss more often than you nail get heavily prioritized, unseen cards get a gentle bump, and sections you haven't marked complete stay near the front.
  • Console-wide study uptime. Any study action counts as today's heartbeat: answering a drill card, verifying an exercise, completing a section, or scoring a mock-exam task. One heartbeat keeps the day green on the dashboard's eight-week strip; miss a day and the streak drops (unlike your Deployments, it won't self-heal). Ten cards a day is the drill's own daily goal, shown right above the session. Streaks are a psychological trick, and they genuinely work.
  • Everything travels with your progress file. Export and Import on the dashboard carry your full drill history; Reset progress wipes it clean.
