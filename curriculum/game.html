CNPE Quest

A role-playing game over the whole curriculum. The overworld has five regions, one per exam domain, and a town for each of the 29 sections. In a town, the people teach you the theory and hand you the commands. Pass the town's trial, a quiz built from its self-check questions, and its dungeon opens: inside is a fault, the kind make break injects, and you fight it with real commands against a simulated cluster. Evidence you surface knocks the enemy down; the right fix finishes it. Every trial answer feeds the drill's own record, and the quest's progress rides your progress file and the optional sync.

The quest

arrows or WASD to walk · enter to act · esc to leave

How it works

theory first, commands second
  • The map is the exam. Five regions for the five domains, all open from the start, with a town for every section. Walk onto a town to enter it; walk onto a dungeon door, a boss keep or the final gate to fight what is inside.
  • Towns teach. Each town has three to five people worth talking to. Their lines are the section's concepts, distilled, and most of them teach a technique: a command family such as kubectl describe or flux get kustomizations that appears in your battle menu from then on. Read opens the section itself; Inn heals; the Shop sells items for gold.
  • Trials are the self-checks. A town's trial is a multiple-choice quiz over that section's own self-check cards, the same cards the drill deals. Seventy percent clears it and opens the town's dungeon. Every answer is recorded into your drill history and counts as a heartbeat for the study streak, so the quest and the drill share one memory.
  • Battles are incidents. The enemy is a fault, presented as the ticket a team would file. You have a terminal. Inspect with a learned technique or type any command; the simulated cluster answers, and a command that surfaces evidence knocks the enemy's guard down and earns experience. Fix with the repair command: the right one with all the evidence found is a critical hit for full reward; the right one guessed early still wins, for less; a wrong one shows you its error and costs you a hit. The enemy strikes every turn, harder in harder dungeons, and typing a command yourself earns a fifth more than picking it from the menu.
  • Items. A Hint Scroll names the next thing to inspect, a Lens reveals one piece of evidence outright, an Elixir restores health, and a Cheat Sheet, one per tool family, permanently shows the syntax of that tool's techniques in the terminal. You start with two scrolls.
  • Bosses. Clearing every dungeon in a region opens its keep, where two faults chain into one battle. Beating all five keeps opens the Exam, the final gate: three faults on a shorter clock.
  • Progress (level, gold, items, techniques, towns cleared, battles won, where you stood) lives in the same store as the rest of the console, so Export, Import and the optional sync carry it, and experience and gold earned in two browsers add up rather than one hiding the other.
