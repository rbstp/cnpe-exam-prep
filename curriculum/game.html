Controls & quick help

Click to travel, or walk with WASD / arrows. Enter acts, Esc goes back or stops travel, Q opens your journal, and F toggles fullscreen. Touch controls work too.

Talk to learn commands → pass the trial → enter the dungeon. In battle, inspect for evidence, then repair the fault. Rest at an inn when health is low.

Progress saves automatically. Sound is optional; reduced motion follows your device settings.
