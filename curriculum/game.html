Controls & quick help

Click anywhere to travel, or move with WASD / arrow keys. Enter interacts, Esc backs out or stops travel, Q opens your journal, and F toggles fullscreen. Touch controls work on mobile too.

Chat with townsfolk to pick up commands → clear the trial → unlock the dungeon. In battle, inspect the cluster for evidence before running the fix. Stop by an inn when your HP runs low.

Progress saves automatically. Audio is opt-in, and reduced motion follows your system preference.
